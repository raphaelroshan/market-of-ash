## Investment assessment

Market of Ash has a credible Frontier-inspired trade foundation and substantially more systemic depth than the opening presentation reveals. The investment question is no longer whether the economy works; it is whether an evaluator can see the game’s identity and complete its core fantasy without encountering clipped presentation or a performance warning.

The full creative vertical must therefore be built before additional breadth is treated as persuasive. It should show first purchase, destination comparison, departure, road consequence, arrival sale or recovery, return-market change, an optional black-market pressure, and a terminal economic/political receipt. Ordinary trade must remain viable and contracts must remain optional.

The complete implementation contract is [`investment_evaluation_roadmap.md`](investment_evaluation_roadmap.md). The first task is **MA-I1: shell and deterministic performance repair**. Do not add more factions, goods, or map regions until the opening is readable and the timing budget is back inside bounds.
