# Market of Ash — Early Access Decision Record

**Decision:** Target Early Access as a small, complete trade-and-travel sandbox rather than a single polished basin scenario or an unfinished continent.

The Five-Well Basin remains the quality anchor. Two additional regions may be visually lighter, but each must contain a functioning economy, route risk, recovery, event consequence, optional contract path, ordinary-trade path, save-safe completion, and a meaningful result. This creates enough breadth for a real Early Access player without requiring final art for every location.

The principal trade-off is **breadth versus polish**. We accept reused UI components, temporary or lightly authored regional art, and compact content counts in the follow-on regions. We do not accept fake markets, decorative settlements, mandatory contract chains, broken save boundaries, unreadable controls, or regions that are only background swaps.

The second trade-off is **authored pressure versus player freedom**. Contracts, faction requests, and crises can create attractive opportunities, but conventional buying and selling must remain valuable. Missed scenarios create replacement actors, shortages, route changes, or alternate endings rather than a forced reload or a dead campaign.

The active implementation order is responsive Basin completion, a second region, a connected third region, replacement-faction breadth, event/crew/replay depth, and release hardening. Human testing may later calibrate the build but is not a prerequisite for implementation. Automated deterministic trials, complete-flow fixtures, layout checks, screenshots, and save/replay coverage are the active gates.
