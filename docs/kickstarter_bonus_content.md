# Market of Ash — Kickstarter Bonus Content

## Recommended archive

Offer a digital **Basin Builder’s Field Journal** containing the versioned screenshots in [`visual_evidence_gallery.md`](visual_evidence_gallery.md), selected design notes, and short commentary on how the Bazaar, routes, events, and arrivals form one deterministic trade-and-travel loop.

The archive should identify the included capture set as `v0.12.0-alpha-quality`. It should present the images as development evidence and design history, not final marketing art.

## Suggested backer-facing copy

> **Basin Builder’s Field Journal:** Follow the development of Market of Ash from its first readable market and route screens through the arrival handoff. This digital archive includes annotated alpha screenshots, economy-design notes, and a look at how ordinary buying and selling, optional contracts, faction pressure, and alternate regional outcomes fit together.

## Guardrails

Do not promise a fixed feature list, final art style, release date, storefront launch, or campaign scope unless separately approved. The screenshot archive should retain the build number and explain that the game remains in internal alpha development. Any later replacement screenshots should be added under a new versioned directory rather than silently overwriting this capture set.
