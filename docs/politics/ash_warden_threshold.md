# Ash Warden Relationship Proof

The first faction threshold is intentionally narrow and commercial.

- Standing is bounded from -10 through +10.
- Below +2, the player is `Unregistered` and pays the full 12-ashmark Toll Road fee.
- At +2 or above, the player is a `Recognized carrier` and pays 9 ashmarks.
- The benefit is shown in the route forecast together with its tradeoff: the caravan travels on a named manifest with greater official visibility.
- Buying Warden ration packs and paying the disputed Gatekeeper toll each add +1 through named command results.
- Tess's ledger challenge subtracts 1 and states the consequence before selection.

No other route, market price, contract, or event chance changes at this threshold.
