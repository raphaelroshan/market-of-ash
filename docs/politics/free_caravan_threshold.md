# Free Caravan Relationship Proof

- Standing is bounded from -10 through +10.
- Below +2, the player is an `Unknown trader` and pays the full 4-ashmark Old Road fee.
- At +2 or above, the player is a `Known road-sharer` and pays 2 ashmarks.
- Fair distribution at The Last Clean Barrel and waiting to identify the Three Riders' sponsor each grant +1 through named event results.
- The forecast states that the informal discount does not reduce the Old Road's 35% cargo risk.

This is the counter-path to Warden recognition: cheaper informal travel on an exposed route rather than cheaper official travel with greater visibility.
