# No Road Owns the Sky — Ending Proof

This deterministic campaign ending is evaluated at crisis stage 3 (day 10 or later), after the shared-relief and Warden-reserve predicates.

Required state:

- Free Caravan standing is at least 2;
- Ash Warden standing is at most 1;
- arms escalation is at most 1.

When all predicates hold, the save records `ending_id = ending_free_caravan_routes` and the authored regional summary. The result names public supply, independent access, contained arms traffic, shared route information, and the caravan's acceptance of volatile margins.

A fresh command-only proof takes medicine over the exposed road, waits to identify the Three Riders' sponsor, shares water at The Last Clean Barrel, and advances the crisis through legal routes. It reaches day 10 without a relief contract, debug grants, weapon sales, or combat.
