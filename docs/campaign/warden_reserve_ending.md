# Order at the Cistern — Ending Proof

The second deterministic campaign ending is evaluated at crisis stage 3 (day 10 or later), after the shared-relief ending has been checked.

Required state:

- Ash Warden standing is at least 3;
- Free Caravan standing is at most 1;
- arms escalation is at most 1.

When all predicates hold, the save records `ending_id = ending_warden_reserve` and the authored regional summary. The result describes uneven public supply, permit-controlled access, contained arms traffic, Warden influence, and the caravan's preference for predictable regulated margins.

A fresh command-only proof earns Warden standing through Ashgate logistics and the Gatekeeper toll, profits from moving medicine to Brine Cross, and advances the crisis through legal routes. It reaches day 10 without requiring relief completion, debug grants, weapon sales, or combat.
