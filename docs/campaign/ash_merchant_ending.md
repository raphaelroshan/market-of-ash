# The Best Margin — Ending Proof

This deterministic campaign ending is evaluated last at crisis stage 3 (day 10 or later).

Required state:

- the caravan holds at least 220 ashmarks;
- Reedwatch resilience is at most 1;
- arms escalation is at most 1.

When all predicates hold and no earlier ending qualifies, the save records `ending_id = ending_ash_merchant` and the authored regional summary. The result names fragile public supply, access based on purchasing power, contained arms traffic, and the player's concentration of profit.

A fresh command-only proof makes repeated medicine runs, pays to protect one exposed load, takes the emergency premium at The Last Clean Barrel, and reaches day 10 above the wealth threshold. It requires no debug grants, weapon sales, or combat.
