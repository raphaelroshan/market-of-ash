# Market of Ash 0.13.1 — Departure Clarity Patch

This prerelease keeps the complete Five-Well Basin vertical slice from 0.13.0 and fixes a first-time-player clarity problem at the Departure Desk.

## Changed

- Every itinerary card now separates the selected cargo plan from the quantity actually held.
- Unloaded and partially loaded plans are visibly marked `IF BOUGHT`; covered plans are marked `READY`.
- Cargo values are labeled `VALUE` rather than implying that forecast cargo is already aboard.
- The departure action explicitly says when the caravan will leave without the planned load or with only part of it.
- Tooltips state the exact cargo shortfall and confirm that empty travel remains legal.

No economy, route, event, save, or command rule changed. The patch only makes the existing hypothetical forecast and actual hold boundary visible at the decision point.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended portable package.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `release_manifest.json`, and `windows-version.json` provide integrity and provenance.

The Windows build remains unsigned and may display a reputation warning. It requires no online account and submits no report automatically.

## Known limitations

- Procedural visuals remain alpha-quality rather than final commercial art.
- Physical-controller feel, Windows high-DPI devices, screen readers, antivirus reputation, and storefront launch behavior need human hardware testing.
- Moderated first-time-player sessions are still required to validate comprehension and enjoyment beyond deterministic and visual checks.
