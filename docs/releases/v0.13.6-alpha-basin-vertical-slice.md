# Market of Ash 0.13.6 — Licensed Interface Audio Patch

This prerelease preserves the complete Five-Well Basin vertical slice and replaces prototype beeps with a restrained temporary sound vocabulary.

## Changed

- Successful commands use a short licensed confirmation cue.
- Blocked commands use a distinct licensed error cue.
- Committed travel uses a brief wagon-like creak.
- The existing Interface Sounds setting still disables all three cues.
- Essential outcomes remain visible in text; sound never carries unique gameplay information.

The selected assets are CC0 Kenney files stored with their license records under `assets/temporary/kenney/`.

No economy, route, event, campaign, save, or command behavior changed.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended portable package.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `release_manifest.json`, and `windows-version.json` provide integrity and provenance.

The Windows build remains unsigned and may display a reputation warning.

## Known limitations

- The cues are temporary licensed assets rather than final bespoke sound design.
- Procedural visuals remain alpha-quality rather than final commercial art.
- Physical-controller feel, Windows high-DPI devices, screen readers, antivirus reputation, and storefront launch behavior need human hardware testing.
