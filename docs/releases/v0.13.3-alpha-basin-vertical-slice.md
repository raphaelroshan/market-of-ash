# Market of Ash 0.13.3 — Bazaar Trade Ticket Patch

This prerelease keeps the complete Five-Well Basin vertical slice while making the Market Stall's core decision easier to scan.

## Changed

- The Bazaar now presents the selected load as a structured trade ticket instead of a dense diagnostic paragraph.
- Cargo and destination lead the card, followed by separate Buy, Sell, Road, and Net values.
- Route risk, provisions, available ashmarks, projected hold use, local supply reason, destination need, and next action remain visible.
- Positive and losing forecasts use distinct value colors without relying on color alone.
- The complete equivalent text remains available to Web accessibility output and playtest reports.

No price, demand, route, event, contract, save, or command rule changed.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended portable package.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `release_manifest.json`, and `windows-version.json` provide integrity and provenance.

The Windows build remains unsigned and may display a reputation warning.

## Known limitations

- Procedural visuals remain alpha-quality rather than final commercial art.
- Physical-controller feel, Windows high-DPI devices, screen readers, antivirus reputation, and storefront launch behavior need human hardware testing.
- Moderated first-time-player sessions are still required to validate comprehension and enjoyment beyond deterministic and visual checks.
