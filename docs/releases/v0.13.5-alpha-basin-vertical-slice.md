# Market of Ash 0.13.5 — Responsive Opening Patch

This prerelease preserves the complete Five-Well Basin vertical slice and repairs the constrained Main Menu and Introduction composition identified by the latest strategy verification.

## Changed

- Main Menu and Introduction now use the stacked composition at 1280 pixels wide and below.
- The compact Main Menu uses a shorter illustrated header so every default action stays inside its card.
- Opening cards add horizontal safety gutters.
- Introduction actions wrap safely in Large Text.
- The 1600×900 and larger opening retains a wider split composition.
- Native evidence now validates every required opening heading, explanation, status, and action against both its card and the active viewport.

No economy, route, event, campaign, save, or command behavior changed.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended portable package.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `release_manifest.json`, and `windows-version.json` provide integrity and provenance.

The Windows build remains unsigned and may display a reputation warning.

## Known limitations

- Procedural visuals remain alpha-quality rather than final commercial art.
- Physical-controller feel, Windows high-DPI devices, screen readers, antivirus reputation, and storefront launch behavior need human hardware testing.
- Moderated first-time-player sessions are still required to validate comprehension and enjoyment beyond deterministic and visual checks.
