# Market of Ash 0.13.11 — Market Coin Audio Patch

This prerelease gives completed Bazaar exchanges a short material coin cue.

## Changed

- Successful purchases and sales play the CC0 Kenney `handleCoins2` cue.
- General commands retain the existing confirmation sound.
- Blocked trades retain the warning sound.
- The existing Interface Sounds setting suppresses the trade cue immediately.
- The export-safe runtime copy records its source and SHA-256 provenance.

No trade calculation, command, receipt, save, route, event, or campaign behavior changed.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended portable package.
- Integrity and provenance files accompany both downloads.

The Windows build remains unsigned and may display a reputation warning.
