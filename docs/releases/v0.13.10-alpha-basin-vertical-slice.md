# Market of Ash 0.13.10 — Journey Consequence Receipt

This prerelease makes the result of a roadside decision immediately scannable before the full arrival report.

## Changed

- Journey results lead with `PLAN HELD`, `RISK AVOIDED`, or `RISK REALIZED`.
- Risk receipts name the exposed cargo and exact deterministic roll versus the disclosed threshold.
- A code-native crate seal reinforces the state without making color or imagery carry unique information.
- The complete comparison and recovery advice remain visible and available to Web accessibility output.
- Native capture coverage now includes the realized-loss receipt at normal and Large Text sizes.

No trade, route, event, campaign, save, or command behavior changed.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended portable package.
- Integrity and provenance files accompany both downloads.

The Windows build remains unsigned and may display a reputation warning.
