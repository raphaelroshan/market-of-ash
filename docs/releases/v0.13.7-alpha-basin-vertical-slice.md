# Market of Ash 0.13.7 — Caravan Departure Dust Patch

This prerelease preserves the complete Five-Well Basin vertical slice and gives committed travel a clearer physical transition.

## Changed

- Animated departures now leave a short, palette-tinted dust wake behind the caravan.
- The effect uses the licensed temporary Kenney particle kit and fades before the mandatory road stop.
- Reduce Travel Motion suppresses the effect completely.
- Native visual verification now records the committed departure separately from the later road stop.
- Release exports now exclude unused raw temporary-asset packs and include only the curated runtime audio/VFX selections.

No economy, route resolution, event, campaign, save, or command behavior changed.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended portable package.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `release_manifest.json`, and `windows-version.json` provide integrity and provenance.

The Windows build remains unsigned and may display a reputation warning.

## Known limitations

- The dust texture is temporary licensed VFX rather than final bespoke caravan animation.
- Procedural visuals remain alpha-quality rather than final commercial art.
- Physical-controller feel, Windows high-DPI devices, screen readers, antivirus reputation, and storefront launch behavior need human hardware testing.
