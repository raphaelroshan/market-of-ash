# Market of Ash 0.13.0 — Basin Vertical Slice

This prerelease is the first complete, downloadable game-quality slice of the Five-Well Basin. Start with a clean caravan, learn the market and road model, make an ordinary trade or optional promise, compare legal itineraries, travel through an in-between road scene, resolve a disclosed encounter, arrive in a changed market, and pursue one of five regional conclusions.

## What is in this build

- A game-facing Main Menu and three-card illustrated introduction.
- Five distinct, data-driven settlement Bazaars with trade, jobs, information/services, crew, outlook, and departure flows.
- Ordinary buying and selling as a complete path; contracts remain optional pressure and reward.
- Side-by-side itinerary comparison for every legal direct journey, including fees, time, provisions, cargo value, risk, forecast confidence, and expected net.
- Mandatory, skippable road observation with reduced-motion support before event or arrival.
- Four deterministic road-event families with visible costs, prerequisites, outcomes, persistence, and later callbacks.
- Adaptive Well Commons content when official relief is ignored or fails, including replacement trade and a distinct ending.
- Five campaign endings and a causal debrief with Continue, Replay, Title, and journey-report actions.
- Versioned saves, migration and backup recovery, Pause, keyboard/controller navigation, remapping, Large text, reduced motion, browser accessibility controls, and privacy-safe JSON playtest reports.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended clean portable package; extract it and run `Market of Ash/market-of-ash.exe`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `release_manifest.json`, and `windows-version.json` provide integrity and provenance.
- The source archive records the exact tagged source used by CI.

Windows may warn because this alpha is not code-signed. The build does not require an online account and does not submit playtest reports automatically.

## Verification

The tagged pipeline runs the complete deterministic suite, exports Windows and Web builds, validates the embedded Windows payload, clean-extracts and launch-smokes the portable ZIP, captures the visible Windows client area, checks version resources, creates a provenance manifest and source snapshot, and verifies every SHA-256 checksum. Pull-request CI also traverses the packaged Web build in Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. Local real-renderer evidence covers 25 states at both 1280×720 and 1600×900, including Large text and the complete journey.

## Known limitations

- Procedural art and motion are alpha-quality and are not final commercial assets.
- The Windows build is unsigned and portable; there is no installer, updater, cloud save, achievement, or Steam/Epic integration.
- Physical-controller feel, Windows high-DPI devices, screen readers, antivirus reputation, and storefront launch behavior need human hardware testing.
- The content covers one basin rather than a full campaign: five settlements, three route families, seven goods, four route-event families, and five endings.
- Automated testing proves deterministic behavior and layout contracts, but moderated first-time-player testing is still needed to measure comprehension and enjoyment.

Please include the build version, seed, and exported journey report with any issue. The game exposes those details without collecting identity or account data.
