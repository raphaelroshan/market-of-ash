# Market of Ash 0.13.8 — Bazaar Trade Receipt Patch

This prerelease preserves the complete Five-Well Basin vertical slice and gives successful market exchanges a clearer game-facing completion beat.

## Changed

- Successful purchases show a stamped receipt with cargo loaded, ashmarks paid, and resulting hold use.
- Successful sales show the corresponding cargo released and ashmarks received.
- Receipts dismiss on Bazaar navigation, departure, Main Menu, or Pause.
- Reduce Travel Motion retains the receipt text while suppressing its scale and fade flourish.
- Native evidence now covers the receipt at normal and Large Text sizes.

Blocked trades retain their existing visible explanation and never show a false success receipt. No economy, price, route, event, campaign, save, or command behavior changed.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended portable package.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `release_manifest.json`, and `windows-version.json` provide integrity and provenance.

The Windows build remains unsigned and may display a reputation warning.

## Known limitations

- The stamped receipt is temporary UI art rather than final bespoke market illustration.
- Physical-controller feel, Windows high-DPI devices, screen readers, antivirus reputation, and storefront launch behavior need human hardware testing.
