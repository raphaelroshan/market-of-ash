# Market of Ash 0.13.2 — Large-Text Route Selection Patch

This prerelease keeps the complete Five-Well Basin vertical slice from 0.13.0 and the departure clarity improvements from 0.13.1, while fixing a minimum-window accessibility regression.

## Changed

- The selected itinerary keeps its explicit `SELECTED` label when Large text is enabled.
- Large-text route cards receive enough vertical space to show selection, costs, cargo planning, risk, and net outcome together.
- Route rows use shorter `DAY` and `PROV` labels so the same information remains readable without excess wrapping.
- The Departure Desk keeps its return and commitment actions pinned below the scrollable route comparison.

No economy, route, event, save, or command rule changed. This patch only preserves decision context at larger text sizes.

## Downloads

- `market-of-ash.exe` is the standalone Windows x86-64 build.
- `market-of-ash-windows.zip` is the recommended portable package.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `release_manifest.json`, and `windows-version.json` provide integrity and provenance.

The Windows build remains unsigned and may display a reputation warning. It requires no online account and submits no report automatically.

## Known limitations

- Procedural visuals remain alpha-quality rather than final commercial art.
- Physical-controller feel, Windows high-DPI devices, screen readers, antivirus reputation, and storefront launch behavior need human hardware testing.
- Moderated first-time-player sessions are still required to validate comprehension and enjoyment beyond deterministic and visual checks.
