# Flash-game ranking research notes

## Scope
Use the broader definition: classic browser games that originally ran in Adobe Flash, including games later preserved or ported to HTML5. Popularity is not a single official statistic, so rank using a transparent composite of historical reach, cross-portal presence, documented play/view counts where available, cultural recognition, longevity, and archival prominence.

## Method caution
Do not present the final order as an official universal ranking. Separate games with measurable portal data from those supported mainly by cross-site presence, historical recognition, and archival/retrospective evidence. Verify dates, original Flash status, and evidence per title before final scoring.

### Happy Wheels — official site
URL: https://totaljerkface.com/

The official Totaljerkface homepage states that the JavaScript port was released in December 2020 as Flash ended, that the database of users, levels, and replays remained the same, and thanks users for still playing Happy Wheels 10 years after launch. This confirms a 2010 Flash origin, unusually long-lived user-generated ecosystem, and successful post-Flash continuity. The official site does not provide a total play count in the retrieved page.

### Ninja Kiwi — official archive
URL: https://ninjakiwi.com/

This supports giving the Bloons family a very high legacy/popularity score, while avoiding conflating later mobile/Steam play totals with the original Flash releases.

### Flashpoint Archive
URL: https://flashpointarchive.org/

Flashpoint describes itself as the world’s largest effort to preserve web-based games, animations, and interactive experiences.
