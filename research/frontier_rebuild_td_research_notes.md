# Frontier / Rebuild / Creative Tower Defense Research Notes

## Frontier-related references

### Frontier — Armor Games
URL: https://armorgames.com/play/4480/frontier

Earlier research records Frontier at 4,089,402 Armor Games plays, 14,674 ratings, and 8,458 favorites. Armor describes 50 cities across 10 environments and a non-traditional RPG centered on trade, travel, and building a fortune. This remains the primary benchmark.

### Merchant of the Skies — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1040070/Merchant_of_the_Skies/

Steam reports Very Positive reviews (1,438 at retrieval), single-player format, and a $14.99 price. Its pitch is to become the captain of a flying ship, sell items, gather resources, and build a trade company in the clouds. Tags include Trading, Base Building, Management, Casual, and Exploration. Useful lesson: make the trade fantasy concrete through a vehicle, route network, and company growth; keep it more accessible than a full simulation.

### Star Traders: Frontiers — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/335620/Star_Traders_Frontiers/

Steam reports 3,252 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $14.99 price, single-player, Workshop, leaderboards, and a 2018 release. It pitches the player as captain of a starship with a customizable crew in a universe of political intrigue, alien threats, exploration, and faction conflict. Useful lesson: crew roles, emergent events, factions, and multiple career identities can create a large systemic RPG from a relatively small number of reusable content pieces. Risk: complexity and UI density can overwhelm players.

## Rebuild-related references

### The Last Spell — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1105670/The_Last_Spell/

Steam reports 6,674 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $24.99 regular price, single-player format, 80 achievements, and multiple DLC expansions. The pitch combines nightly tactical combat against monsters with daytime rebuilding of city defenses in a roguelite tactical RPG. Useful lesson: alternate management and defense phases create a strong cadence, while run-based progression and DLC can support a premium product. It is more combat-heavy and less relationship-driven than Rebuild.

### Against the Storm — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1336490/Against_the_Storm/

Steam reports 18,958 Overwhelmingly Positive English reviews at retrieval, a 2023 release, a demo, single-player format, and Steam Deck Verified status. It is a dark-fantasy city-builder roguelite where players rebuild civilization through multiple settlements, species, resource chains, and hostile conditions. Useful lesson: a campaign layer that carries resources/upgrades between short settlements can make a city-builder more replayable and reduce the commitment of a single endless map. Risk: the formula is already well-served, so a Rebuild-like product should lean harder into named survivors, relationships, and moral consequences.

## Creative tower-defense references

### Sir, We Have an Orc Problem — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/4594150/Sir_We_Have_an_Orc_Problem/

Steam reports 2,244 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $9.99 price, single-player format, and a July 28, 2026 release. It is an incremental-style tower defense with overwhelming orc hordes, bullets, bombs, and lasers; its tags include Tower Defense, Incremental, War, Roguelite, and Military. Useful lesson: exaggerate scale and firepower, make the player feel like an active commander, and pair wave survival with persistent upgrades. Its current success also shows that a very focused variation can be commercially viable.

### There Are No Orcs — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3480990/There_Are_No_Orcs/

Steam reports 1,174 all-time Very Positive reviews, a $8.99 price, single-player format, and a November 6, 2025 release. It combines RTS, roguelike, auto-battler, tower defense, and city-builder elements. Players place tiles/buildings for production synergies, generate armies, select among three factions, and choose among 30 commanders with active skills, passive traits, and exclusive buildings. Useful lesson: replace conventional tower placement with a spatial economy and auto-produced armies; make each run an experiment in build synergies.

### Booster Pack Heroes — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/4618570/Booster_Pack_Heroes/

Steam lists the game as coming soon with no reviews at retrieval. It is a short single-player incremental game in which players open booster packs containing heroes and spells, survive monster hordes, upgrade their collection, and conquer chapters. Useful lesson: turn the tower-defense roster into a collectible reveal loop. Commercial risk: random booster-pack mechanics can feel extractive, so a premium version should use deterministic unlocks, earned packs, or clearly disclosed non-monetized collection systems.

### Ratropolis — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1108370/Ratropolis/

Steam reports 1,711 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $17.99 price, and single-player, workshop-free but replayable systems. It explicitly fuses roguelite, tower defense, city building, and deck building in real time. Useful lesson: cards can serve as a flexible content and progression layer on top of defense; the city’s production economy creates meaningful decisions beyond damage-per-second optimization.

### Orcs Must Die! 3 — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1522820/Orcs_Must_Die_3/

Steam reports 4,946 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $29.99 price, single-player and online co-op, and a 2021 release. It combines third-person shooter combat with trap placement, massive War Scenarios, mountable War Machines, Weekly Challenges, Endless Mode, and a Scramble mode in which enemies evolve and players collect modifiers. Useful lesson: direct hero control can make tower defense feel active and spectacular; special large-scale modes can sell the fantasy beyond ordinary lanes. It is already a mature commercial benchmark, so a new game needs a more unusual control or simulation hook.

### Dungeon Warfare 2 — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/698540/Dungeon_Warfare_2/

Steam reports 1,119 Very Positive reviews at retrieval, a $14.99 regular price, single-player format, Workshop support, and 2018 release. Its design centers on physics-enabled tower-defense mayhem, 33 unique traps with eight traits each, more than 30 enemies with special traits, and environmental hazards. Useful lesson: the most interesting TD decisions can be about manipulating space, momentum, and enemy behavior rather than merely selecting damage towers.

### Isle of Arrows — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1946970/Isle_of_Arrows/

Steam reports 935 Very Positive reviews at retrieval, a $12.99 price, and single-player format. It fuses board-game tile placement with tower defense: randomly drawn tiles expand the island, build paths, place towers, and create a new strategic puzzle each run. Campaigns unlock elements, while modes, guilds, modifiers, and challenges extend replayability. Useful lesson: make the board itself the resource and the puzzle; random offers should be constrained enough to create adaptation rather than frustration.

### Dome Keeper — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1637320/Dome_Keeper/

Steam reports 11,609 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a 2022 release, single-player mode, and a wide multiplayer feature set including co-op, PvP, cross-platform multiplayer, and Workshop. Its core loop alternates mining for resources and gadgets with urgently returning to defend the dome against alien waves. Useful lesson: alternating offense/economy and defense phases creates natural time pressure and makes upgrades feel earned. It is a strong reference for a Rebuild or tower-defense concept with a second activity rather than a conventional static lane map.

### Bad North: Jotunn Edition — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/688420/Bad_North_Jotunn_Edition/

Steam reports 5,600 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $14.99 regular price, and a single-player real-time tactics roguelite format. It asks the player to defend procedurally shaped islands against Viking invaders and exploit each island’s geometry. Useful lesson: small maps with unique terrain can generate high decision density and readable runs; this is a strong reference for a Rebuild-like “defend one district/island at a time” structure.

### Creeper World 4 — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/848480/Creeper_World_4/

Steam reports 3,839 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $24.99 regular price, single-player and online/LAN co-op, Workshop, a level editor, and a built-in mission generator. Its enemy is a physics-based fluid that floods 3D terrain, while player-made missions, custom units, and custom modes extend the game. Useful lesson: redesigning the enemy as a spatial process rather than a stream of discrete units can make tower defense feel like territory control and engineering.

### Recettear: An Item Shop’s Tale — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/70400/Recettear_An_Item_Shops_Tale/

Steam reports 5,523 Overwhelmingly Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $19.99 price, and a single-player loop combining market purchases, pricing, shop interior management, randomized dungeons, multiple adventurers, Survival Mode, Endless Mode, and debt repayment. Useful lesson: Frontier’s trade system can become more emotionally legible when attached to a concrete obligation, companion relationships, and a complementary action activity. It is a reference rather than a competing Frontier remake.

### Curious Expedition 2 — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1040230/Curious_Expedition_2/

Steam reports 586 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $19.99 regular price, and single-player format. It is a turn-based narrative roguelike using procedural gameplay and story to create unique expeditions; it also has multiple DLC regions. Useful lesson: route choices, supplies, crew composition, and authored event fragments can make each journey feel personal without requiring a fully simulated open world.

### Moonlighter — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/606150/Moonlighter/

Steam reports 7,253 Very Positive English reviews at retrieval, a $19.99 price, and single-player format. It explicitly splits the player’s routine between an action-RPG/roguelite adventure and shopkeeping. Useful lesson: alternating “go out and acquire” with “return and monetize” gives trading a satisfying rhythm; for Frontier, the equivalent could be travel/exploration followed by market, crew, and town development.
