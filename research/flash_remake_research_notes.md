# Commercial remake research notes

## User benchmark: Frontier and TRADESMAN

### Original Frontier — Armor Games
URL: https://armorgames.com/play/4480/frontier

Extracted Armor Games metadata reports 4,089,402 plays, 14,674 ratings, 8,458 favorites, and publication on Sep. 9, 2009. The description calls Frontier a non-traditional RPG that mostly takes the form of an economics game. The player travels between 50 cities across 10 environments, trades, earns money, chooses whether to fight the Buccaneers or join the Enforcers, and follows an open-ended path. The page also reports 50 achievements and notes that the game is large enough to request additional storage. This is unusually strong evidence of a commercially legible core loop despite relatively simple presentation.

### Original Frontier — Newgrounds
URL: https://www.newgrounds.com/portal/view/513305

The search-indexed Newgrounds page describes Frontier as an “Oregon Trail style RPG/Trading game” with 50 cities. Live browser access was blocked by a challenge screen, so Newgrounds is treated as corroborating the concept and distribution, not as a source of precise statistics.

### TRADESMAN: Deal to Dealer — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2555430/TRADESMAN_Deal_to_Dealer/

The Steam page reports release on June 18, 2024, Early Access status, 1,431 all-time reviews marked “Mostly Positive,” and 41 recent reviews marked “Mixed” at the time of retrieval. It is listed as single-player and includes Steam Workshop, achievements, Steam Cloud, and family sharing. The store page describes trading, automatic battles with active skills, goods with traits, mercenary recruitment, consumables, talents, transport quests, multiple regions, and a planned full release on Aug. 27, 2026. Listed price at retrieval was $2.49 USD. The evidence does not support calling the game a commercial failure; the more accurate diagnosis is that it has positive historical reception but mixed recent reception and a very low-price positioning. User comments/search snippets mention grind and confusion, which should be treated as anecdotal product-feedback signals rather than audited market data.

## Working commercial-remake criteria

Score each candidate on a 100-point basis: 25% clarity and depth of the core loop; 20% room for modern production/value uplift; 20% audience evidence; 15% scope and execution risk; 10% monetization fit for premium single-player or optional multiplayer; and 10% IP/licensing feasibility. IP feasibility is a risk flag, not legal clearance. Games based on Nintendo, Valve, Disney, Warner, or other third-party properties should be marked high-risk even if their historical audience is strong.

## Candidate universe

Prioritize original or creator-owned Flash-era games from the earlier top-50: Frontier, Kingdom Rush, Bloons, The Last Stand, Raze, Warfare 1917/1944, Crush the Castle, Fancy Pants, Desktop Tower Defense, Motherload, Fireboy and Watergirl, Papa’s Freezeria/Pizzeria, GemCraft, Strike Force Heroes, Sonny, Stick War, Super Smash Flash 2, The World’s Hardest Game, The Visitor, Age of War, Gunblood, Toss the Turtle, Hobo, Line Rider, Alien Hominid, Madness: Project Nexus, Raft Wars, Bloxorz, Gold Miner, Bubble Tanks, Burrito Bison, Learn to Fly, and Henry Stickmin.

Exclude or downgrade games whose core value depends on unlicensed IP, whose mechanics are too thin to support a premium remake without radical expansion, or whose historical popularity mainly came from an existing brand rather than the original game design.

## Commercial treatment benchmark

### Adobe Flash Games Steam curator
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/curator/41097579-Adobe-Flash-Games/list/94283

The curator explicitly tracks Flash games that received continuations rather than simple Flash ports. The page itself is dynamically loaded and did not expose all entries, but it confirms that a meaningful comparison set exists: commercial successors, remasters, and sequels should be considered before recommending a new remake.

### Warfare Legacy Collection
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2745870/Warfare_Legacy_Collection/

Released Apr. 28, 2025 by Con Artist Games and published by Armor Games Studios. Steam reports 1,097 all-time reviews marked Very Positive and 87 recent reviews marked Very Positive at retrieval. The $5.79 product combines remasters of Warfare 1917 and Warfare 1944 with enhanced visuals, full-screen support, custom battles, and a new Battle of the Bulge campaign. It is single-player. This is evidence that the Warfare concept has already been commercially refreshed successfully; recommend a substantially new Warfare sequel only if there is a clear design or production gap.

## Candidate evidence

### Raze — Kongregate
URL: https://www.kongregate.com/en/games/addisonr/raze

Kongregate reports a 4.3 rating and 47.0M plays for Raze, released Mar. 8, 2010. It was created by Justin Goncalves and Addison Rodomista, with JuiceTin as collaborator. The game is a single-player 2D action/platform shooter with alien, robot, and zombie enemies, campaign missions, weapons, achievements, and human/alien factions. A developer update says Raze won best shooter at the 2011 Flash Gaming Summit. The original’s strongest commercial opportunity is a polished solo campaign plus optional asynchronous challenges or co-op; avoid launching as a generic competitive live-service shooter.

### Desktop Tower Defense — Kongregate
URL: https://www.kongregate.com/en/games/preecep/desktop-tower-defense

Kongregate reports a 4.2 rating and 5.2M plays for Desktop Tower Defense, released Apr. 4, 2007. The description identifies Paul Preece as developer and describes a grid-like desktop tower-defense puzzle with creep pathing, tower purchase, upgrades, selling, multiple tower types, and difficulty settings. Comments include a request for a non-Flash version, a useful qualitative signal that preservation alone was insufficient for fans. A modern remake could add campaign structure, procedural maps, build variety, accessibility, and Steam Workshop without losing the minimalist placement/pacing identity.

### Sonny — existing Steam remake
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/586750/Sonny/

The Steam page markets Sonny as an all-new adventure that is neither a sequel nor a simple remake, based on the beloved Flash RPG series played by millions. Its differentiators are turn-based combat, combinable skills, party members, equipment upgrades, and a story about zombies and conspiracies. This proves the concept translates to commercial premium RPG form, but it also means a new project should not simply remake Sonny 1: it would need a licensed sequel, a faithful high-quality remake, or a distinct spiritual successor. Search results and community commentary indicate the 2017 version had visual/reception criticism; treat that as anecdotal unless separately quantified.

### Existing-product exclusions/discounts

Commercial successors already substantially cover several otherwise attractive candidates: Super Fancy Pants Adventure, Super Motherload, Sonny, Strike Force Heroes, the Last Stand Legacy Collection/Aftermath, and Warfare Legacy Collection. These should be used as market evidence and quality benchmarks, but not treated as open white-space remake opportunities unless the recommendation is explicitly for a superior sequel or licensed reboot.

### Mutilate-a-Doll 2 — Kongregate
URL: https://www.kongregate.com/en/games/0rava/mutilate-a-doll-2

Kongregate reports a 4.6 rating and 47.0M plays for Mutilate-a-Doll 2, released Oct. 31, 2013. The creator is 0rava. It is a physics/ragdoll sandbox with an overwhelming item library, and the page links to an official downloadable version on itch.io and Steam. The creator’s update says it ranked #2 overall and #1 in Physics, Sandbox, and Ragdoll on Kongregate. This is strong audience evidence but not white space: an official commercial/downloadable version already exists, so a new project would need to be a licensed successor or a clearly differentiated physics-creation game.

### Rebuild — official site
URL: https://rebuildgame.com/

The official site identifies Sarah Northway/Northway Games as the creator and states that Rebuild 3: Gangs of Deadsville is playable on PC, Mac, iOS, and Android. It also documents Steam Workshop support, making the series another example of a successful commercial transition already completed. A new opportunity would be a licensed Rebuild 4, a major sequel, or a spiritual successor rather than a simple remake.

### The Visitor — Newgrounds
URL: https://www.newgrounds.com/portal/view/404463

Newgrounds reports 1,668,505 views, 5,062 favorites, 4,942 votes, a 4.60/5 score, and inclusion in 245 lists. The author is Zeebarf. The game is an interactive horror point-and-click adventure, was front-paged, won Weekly Users’ Choice, and placed third monthly in October 2007. The author’s update says it was also made available as a free iOS/Android download. These metrics support a strong concept/audience case, while the mobile version means a new product should be a larger, premium narrative horror adventure rather than a simple port.

### King’s League II — existing successor
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/996080/Kings_League_II/

Search and Steam page evidence show that Kurechii already released King’s League II as a commercial sequel to the award-winning strategy simulation RPG. It offers 30+ fighter classes, recruitment, training, facility upgrades, tactical battles, story/classic modes, quests, and dungeons. This makes King’s League a benchmark or licensing opportunity, not a clean remake white space.

### Fireboy & Watergirl — existing commercial coverage

Search results identify several current Steam entries in the series, including Fireboy & Watergirl: Elements, Fairy Tales, and newer releases of The Crystal Temple and The Light Temple. The concept remains commercially viable, especially for local/remote co-op, but a new project would need a license and a differentiated presentation or original spiritual-successor characters.

### Fireboy & Watergirl: Elements — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1003480/Fireboy__Watergirl_Elements/

Steam reports 1,095 all-time reviews marked Very Positive and 17 recent reviews marked Very Positive. It is a $0.99 single-player/multiplayer title with shared/split screen and Remote Play Together, released Jan. 24, 2019 by Oslo Albet. This validates demand for the co-op format but suggests the opportunity is a higher-production-value sequel or original successor, not a direct remake at a premium price.

### Strike Force Heroes — Steam
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1981820/Strike_Force_Heroes/

Steam reports 4,174 total reviews, 1,407 English reviews marked Very Positive, and 53 recent reviews marked Mixed at retrieval. It is a commercial 2D arena shooter with single-player, online PvP, and online co-op; the page lists a $10.49 price at retrieval and DLC. The original Flash concept has therefore already been commercially attempted. Recommend only a substantially better sequel/reboot with a strong campaign, modern netcode, and sustainable multiplayer population—not a simple remake.

### Stick RPG — Newgrounds and Steam
Newgrounds original: https://www.newgrounds.com/portal/view/231391
Steam successor: https://store.steampowered.com/app/307640/Stick_RPG_2_Directors_Cut/

Search indexing identifies Stick RPG Complete as an XGen Studios title and the original Stick RPG Newgrounds page as having passed one million views. The Newgrounds page itself was challenge-protected during retrieval. Steam’s Stick RPG 2: Director’s Cut page reports 954 all-time reviews marked Very Positive, a $10.49 price, and a rich single-player life-sim/RPG loop: work, fight, study, eat, drink, gamble, train, sleep, quest, collect, invest, build stats/net worth, explore careers, and solve mysteries. This is strong evidence that the life-sim format can support premium sales, but the IP is already owned and commercially represented by XGen; recommend only a licensed sequel or spiritual successor.
